Sharing

Work on one budget with other people, each with their own level of access.

  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Your own budget is private and always stays that way. To share, create a shared budget and invite people to that — nothing you have already recorded moves into it.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Owner controls everything, including members and savings. Editor records income and expenses but cannot move money in or out of savings funds. Viewer can look and nothing more.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} {{if .MailEnabled}} An invitation is emailed to them, and also appears as a banner the next time they sign in. It expires in 24 hours.
    • {{else}} There is no email server configured, so an invitation appears as a banner the next time that person signs in. Tell them to expect it. {{end}}
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Use the picker in the top bar to move between your own budget and any shared ones. Whatever is selected there is what Dashboard and Transactions show.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Removing someone leaves everything they entered in place — the entries belong to the budget, not to them, so your totals do not change.

Invite someone

{{if .MailEnabled}}

They get an email. It says who invited them, which budget, and what access they will have — and asks them to sign in with this address. Invitations expire after 24 hours; use Resend if one lapses.

There is no accept link in the email on purpose. A link that granted access on click would be a credential sitting in an inbox, forwardable to anyone. Signing in first means the person who joins is the person who owns the address.

{{else}}

Nothing is emailed. No mail server is configured, so the invitation waits here and appears as a banner the next time that person signs in. Let them know to look for it.
