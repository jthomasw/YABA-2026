Add Expense

Record money going out — either by typing it in, or from a photo of a receipt.

  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Manual is quickest for one item. Upload reads a photo or PDF of a receipt for you — amount, shop, and even individual items — while you watch.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Always double-check what was read against the picture next to it. It's a best guess, not a fact — nothing is saved to your records until you review it and press Save.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Close the tab part-way through and nothing is lost: the receipt keeps processing and waits for you under Receipts.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Choose One-time for a single purchase, or Recurring for something that repeats, like a subscription. Set how often — days, weeks or months — and YABA records it for you automatically from then on, catching up on anything owed each time you open the page.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} What? is the category, and it drives the spending chart. Who, Where and Why are optional — handy if you look back in a few months.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} Mark it essential or not — that's just needs versus wants. Your essential spending is what sets your Emergency Fund target, so it's worth getting right.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} One shop trip covering several categories, like food and household items in one grocery run? Use Break this into items so each part counts under its own category.
  • {{template "icon" "check-circle"}} See the breakdown later on the Monthly Expenses tab, or the full list under Transactions.
