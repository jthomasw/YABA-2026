What is YABA?

YABA — Yet Another Budgeting App — tracks what comes in, what goes out, and whether the money you have is enough for the bills you have coming.

It plans, not just records

Most trackers tell you what you spent last month. YABA also asks whether this month works. You list your recurring expenses — rent, car payment, water, electricity — and rank them by which must be paid first. When income arrives, it is allocated down that list from the top until either everything is covered or the money runs out.

That is the useful part: you find out you are short before the direct debit bounces, and you know exactly which bill is the one at risk.

Fixed and variable bills

Rent is the same every month, so you type it in once. A water bill is not, so YABA works out what it usually costs from the payments you record against it and budgets for that instead.

An emergency fund that is actually sized

Mark the recurring expenses you could not live without as essential. YABA adds them up and uses the total to size your emergency fund target, then tells you how many months you are covered for — and, if you have been dipping into it, how long the balance will last at that rate.

Income that varies

If your income is not the same every month, a single average is misleading. YABA gives a range instead — roughly what to expect, based on the spread of what you have actually earned.

Receipts

Photograph a receipt and carry on. It is processed in the background, and you get a quiet notification when it is ready. If something goes wrong you are told, even if that means waiting until the next time you sign in.

Your data

Everything is stored in a single file on the machine running the app. There is no third party involved, and nothing is sent anywhere.
